About us

It started with us.

Saffra was founded by a couple who found something rare with each other and wanted more couples to feel it.

This is the long version of why we are doing this. It takes about four minutes to read.

Chapter one

The feeling.

We will say the obvious part first. We are a couple, and we are close.

From early on, what we had was more than attraction. It was attention. We noticed each other. We made time when there was none. We learned that intimacy is mostly small things: a hand on a back in the kitchen, a look across a room, a real question at the end of a long day.

Being wanted by someone who truly sees you changes how you move through everything else. You are kinder at work. You are more patient with family. You are braver. We came to believe this feeling is one of the best things two people can give each other, and that it deserves to be looked after like anything else that matters.

Intimacy is mostly small things.

Chapter two

What we saw around us.

Then we looked around, and we kept hearing the same story from couples in good relationships. We are fine. We are just tired.

Work, children, phones, money, stress. Nobody had stopped loving anybody. They had stopped having room. Desire had not gone anywhere. It had been crowded out, a little more each month, until it felt like something that used to happen.

That stayed with us. What we had did not feel like luck. It felt like something we chose every day. We started asking what it would take to help other couples choose it too.

Chapter three

Why a gummy, of all things.

We are not doctors, and a gummy is not a relationship. We want to be clear about that before anything else.

But we do believe in rituals. A small thing you do together every day becomes a signal: this matters to us. So we went looking for something we could take ourselves, and we were disappointed by what we found. One kind of product promised a transformed night in thirty minutes. The other listed a dozen ingredients and would not say how much of any of them was inside.

We wanted the opposite. A short list of ingredients. The amounts researchers have studied in people. A label that tells you everything. And a promise small enough to be true.

Nobody was making that, so we decided to.

Chapter four

How we are building it.

Slowly, and with people who know more than we do.

With doctors

A medical advisory board in men's health, women's health and pharmacy reviews every formula, dose and warning before anything is made.

With real doses

If an ingredient will not fit in two gummies at the amount researchers tested, we leave it out. That is why the list is short.

With open results

Every batch goes to an independent lab. The report is posted by lot number, so you can check your own jar.

What we believe

Five things we hold to.

Desire is built

It is something two people make, on purpose, over time.

Both people count

So there is one for her, one for him, and a box for two.

Honesty is attractive

Every amount on the label. No blends. No miracle claims.

Small beats dramatic

A better month matters more than one big night.

The gummy is the easy part

Time and attention do the real work. We just give you a reason to start.

A reason to stop for a moment, together.

Chapter five

What Saffra is not.

It is not a fix for a relationship that is hurting. It is not a treatment for a medical problem. If something feels wrong in your body, please see your doctor, and if something feels wrong between you, please talk to each other or to someone trained to help.

It will not work in thirty minutes. The research behind our ingredients looked at daily use over weeks, and that is the only promise we are willing to make.

A note to you.

If you are reading this with someone in mind, that is already the point. You are thinking about them. Tell them. Take two gummies if you like, put your phones in another room, and ask each other one real question tonight.

We made Saffra because what we share has made our lives better, and we think more people deserve to feel that. Thank you for being here.
