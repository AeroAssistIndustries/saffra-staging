Questions

Good to know.

How fast does it work?

Saffra is a daily supplement. The studies on its ingredients ran four to twelve weeks, so give it at least a month of daily use.

What is the difference between the two?

For Her pairs saffron with ashwagandha, both studied in women. For Him pairs saffron with fenugreek, which was studied in men, and adds zinc.

Who should not take it?

Adults only. Do not use if you are pregnant or nursing. Talk to your doctor first if you take any medication or have a medical condition, including diabetes, thyroid, liver, heart or blood pressure conditions.

What does lab tested mean?

An independent lab tests each batch for the active amounts, heavy metals, microbes and undeclared drug ingredients. You can look up the report by the lot number on your jar.

Should we both take the same one?

No. Each of you takes your own formula. The For Two set has one jar of each.

What does it taste like?

Blood orange with a little honey. The gummy is pectin based, so it is soft and not sticky.

Is it vegan?

Yes. No gelatin, no artificial colors or flavors.

How long does a jar last?

Thirty days. Each jar holds 60 gummies and the serving is two a day.

Can I take it with other supplements or medication?

Check with your doctor or pharmacist first. Herbs can interact with medicines, including antidepressants, blood thinners and diabetes medication.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime from your account, in two clicks. You can also skip a month.

Our story

Why we started.

We are Sarvesh and Daniela. When we looked at what was being sold for intimacy, we found two kinds of product. One kind promised a transformed night in thirty minutes. The other listed a dozen ingredients and would not say how much of any of them was inside.

We wanted something we would take ourselves: a short list of ingredients, at the amounts researchers have studied, checked by doctors and tested by a lab that does not work for us.

We also wanted it to be for both people. Desire is something couples build together, so we made a formula for her, a formula for him, and a box that holds one of each.

Saffra will not fix a relationship, and we will never say it will. It is two gummies and a reason to stop for a moment each day. The rest is up to the two of you.
